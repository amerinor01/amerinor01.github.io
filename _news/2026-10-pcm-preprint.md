New preprint out: [PCM: Programmable Congestion Management for Terabit RDMA NICs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13130), a portable programming model for congestion control and load balancing on datacenter NICs, evaluated on NVIDIA ConnectX-8, Microsoft 1P, UCCL and an Ultra Ethernet simulator.
