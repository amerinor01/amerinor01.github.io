[INASim](https://github.com/UCLM-RAAP/inasim) (Interconnects Agile Network Simulator) is an interconnection network simulator developed by the [RAAP group](https://github.com/UCLM-RAAP) at the University of Castilla-La Mancha.

I use and develop INASim in my research to model and evaluate congestion management, flow control and congestion isolation mechanisms in high-performance and data-center interconnection networks.

The source code, build instructions and dependencies are available on [GitHub](https://github.com/UCLM-RAAP/inasim).
